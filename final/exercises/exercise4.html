Exercise F4: Final Project Submission - Question and Answering (Week 17)

  • This final project has been the most challenging experience for me, as it involved the full implementation of our Educational Question and Answering system using the final BERT model. During the process, I learned the importance of selecting a suitable pre-trained BERT model for question answering. The BERT-base-uncased model greatly streamlined our workflow because it is already optimized for Q&A pipelines. In addition, we fine-tuned the model using our annotated dataset, which contains machine learning–related knowledge. Through this fine-tuning process, the model was effectively adapted and specialized for our specific task—Educational Question and Answering within the education domain.
  • After fine-tuning the selected model, we proceeded to enhance its performance by applying the optimal hyperparameters identified in our previous activities, such as the number of epochs, batch size, and learning rate. This hyperparameters played a crucial role in improving the accuracy and reliability of our Question and Answering system. By carefully adjusting and validating these configurations, we were able to ensure that the model performed efficiently and delivered more precise results.
  • Moreover, I gained a deeper understanding of how BERT accurately identifies the start and end positions of answers within a given contetxt. During the final implementation, I created and applied custom helper functions, specifically preparing semantic labels and tokenizing and aligning it to ensure that BERT could correctly align the annotated answers with their corresponding token positions.
  • Through the preparting semantic labels stage, I learned how to enhance answer span detection by combinationg exact string matching with semantic similarity techniques using SentenceTransformer. This allowerd the model to locate answers even when the wording did not match perfectly. Meanwhile, the tokenize and align stage helped me understand how BERT handles long context using a sliding window, and how token offsets are mapped to the correct start and end positions. By aligning these positions precisely, the model can be trained more effectively for Question and Answering.
  • To assess the effectives of our Question and Answering system, I learned to apply difference performance metrics such as training loss, validation loss, F1 Score, Exact Match, and Average Inference Time. Through this process, I realized how important these metrics are in determining whether a model is reliable and accurate enough for real use. This helped me appreciate the value of proper model evaluation, and it gave me more confidence in understanding how to evaluate the quality of our QnA system before deploying it in actual educational tasks.
  • Reflecting on this, I gained a valuable knowledge about how systematic evaluation and hyperparameter tuning directly affect model performance, reliability, and usability. These skills are highly relevant to my future carrer as an IT professional, especially in roles related to ML Engineering, Data Engineering, or Data Scienctist. Being able to assess models throughly, optimize them efficiently, and balance performance with resource constraints is essential for deploying production-ready machine learning systems. Moreover, this experience strengthened my problem-solving skills and ability to adapt techniques to real-world constraints, which are critical competencies in any IT career.
