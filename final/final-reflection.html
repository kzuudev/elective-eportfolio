Final Term Reflection

  • Throughout this midterm, I gained a comprehensive understanding of how Natural Language Processing (NLP), machine learning models, and Large Language Models (LLMs) work together to transform raw text into meaningful insights. Working with tasks such as Sentiment Analysis, Text Classification, POS Tagging, and Text Summarization helped me appreciate how important preprocessing, model selection, and evaluation metrics are in building reliable NLP applications. These activities strengthened my foundational skills not only in Python programming but also in understanding how textual data must be cleaned, structured, and evaluated before meaningful results can be produced. This experience gave me a clearer perspective on how NLP models operate and how they can be applied to real-world scenarios in areas like recommendation systems, feedback analysis, and automated content generation.
  • My practical experience with BERT and transformer models deepened my understanding of how modern AI systems interpret and answer questions. Comparing models such as BERT-base-cased, DistilBERT, and Electra taught me that model performance depends not only on accuracy but also on inference time and contextual understanding. I learned how positional encoding, multi-head attention, and feed-forward layers allow BERT to capture bidirectional context, which makes it far more capable than traditional machine learning algorithms. More importantly, the evaluation metrics such as Exact Match, F1-Score, and inference speed helped me realize the importance of quantitative assessment when validating model reliability. These skills are essential for future roles in data analysis, machine learning engineering, or AI development.
  • Finally, exploring Large Language Models, LangChain, and Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) helped me understand the limitations of AI systems, such as hallucination, factuality errors, poor calibration, and reasoning inconsistencies and how modern retrieval-based methods address these issues. By grounding responses in retrieved, verified documents, RAG provides a more trustworthy and contextually accurate output, which is crucial for real-world applications. Learning how LangChain integrates components like embeddings, retrievers, vector databases, and prompt orchestration also exposed me to the engineering side of AI system development. Reflecting on these insights, I realized that the knowledge I gained in this midterm goes beyond academic exercises it equips me with practical skills that are highly relevant to my future career in AI, software development, and data-driven solutions. I now feel more prepared to build systems that are not only functional but also reliable, explainable, and aligned with user needs.
