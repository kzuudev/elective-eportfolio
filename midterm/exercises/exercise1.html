Exercise M1: Exercise M1: Midterm Eportfolio (Week 7)

  • In this exercise about Implementing and Evaluating NLP Application Models using Python, I learned the Natural Language Preprocessing Workflow Pipeline. The process begins with Raw Text, which consists of movie overviews and reviews collected from the datasets. These texts first undergo Text Preprocessing, which includes steps such as lowercasing, and special character removal, stopword removal, and, tokenization to ensure the quality of the data, After preprocessing, different NLP models was trained, including Text Classification, Sentiment Analysis, Part-of-Speech Tagging, and Text Summarization. Finally, the performance of each model was evaluated using the standard metrics such as accuracy, precision, f1-score, and recall and confusion matrix.
  • Moreover, I acquired knowledge in implementation of four NLP task such as Text Classification, Sentiment Analysis, Part-of-Speech Tagging, and Text Summarization. Through the hands-on experience with these tasks, I learned how to preprocess textural data, apply machine learning models, and evaluate their performance across different NLP applications.
  • In Sentiment Analysis, this task was the easiest NLP for me. The objective of this NLP technique was used to determine the sentiment of text, identifying whether the expressed opinion is positive or negative. In this exercise, the model was applied to classify movie reviews based on the sentiment they conveyed.
  • Along with Text Classification, where I applied it to automatically assign text into predefined categories. In this exercise I was applied it to classify movie genres (e.g., Action, Adventure, Drama) based on descriptive overviews of movies.
